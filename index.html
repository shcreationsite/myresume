About OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)

OEE is a metric measuring the efficiency of a production asset (e.g., tire manufacturing machine). Calculated by combining:

  • Availability: How often the asset functions when needed.
  • Performance: How much the asset produces relative to its potential.
  • Quality: How many high-quality items the asset produces.
